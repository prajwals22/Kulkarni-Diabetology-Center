Our Story

Advanced Care with
Uncompromising Ethics

Welcome to Kulkarni Diabetology Center — a dedicated center for Diabetes, Obesity, and Metabolic disorders. Founded in 2024 by Dr. Awani Paithankar Kulkarni, our centre was built on one simple promise: to deliver advanced care with uncompromising ethics.

We believe that true health is achieved when advanced medical treatment works hand-in-hand with positive lifestyle choices. We don't just manage conditions — we partner with you to build sustainable habits, integrating the latest medical science with practical, real-world guidance to help you take control of your long-term wellbeing.

Our Core Values

What Sets Us Apart

Every decision we make is guided by four unwavering principles that define who we are and how we care for every patient.

Science-Backed Practice

Every management plan and recommendation at Kulkarni Diabetology Center is rooted in the latest clinical guidelines and peer-reviewed research — never in fleeting health fads or commercial interests.

A Focus on Lifestyle Integration

The most powerful tool in managing diabetes and metabolic health is your daily routine. Our consultations go beyond prescriptions — creating personalised, sustainable lifestyle strategies with meaningful dietary and activity guidance that fits your life.

Cutting-Edge Technology

Our clinic is equipped with state-of-the-art diagnostic technology. We use these tools not just for diagnosis, but to provide you with clear, actionable feedback on how your lifestyle choices are impacting your health — in real time.

Personalised Nutrition & Health Pathways

Your metabolic health is unique. We dedicate significant time to understanding your history, habits, and goals — designing holistic plans that emphasise customised nutrition, manageable exercise, and mindful habits, because lasting health is built on personalised daily choices.

Our Facility

State-of-the-Art Infrastructure

Our clinic is equipped with the latest diabetological technology — designed to provide maximum comfort, safety, and clinical precision for every patient.

CGM & Glucose Monitoring Suite

Latest continuous glucose monitoring systems for real-time blood sugar tracking, pattern analysis, and precision therapy adjustments — with full setup and training provided.

Advanced Metabolic Diagnostics

In-house HbA1c analyser, lipid panel, thyroid function, insulin resistance, and comprehensive metabolic workup — results available the same day for faster clinical decisions.

Insulin Pump & Therapy Room

Dedicated room for insulin pump initiation, training, and management — ensuring patients and families are confident with their device and regimen from day one.

Diabetic Foot Care Unit

Specialised foot examination bay with monofilament testing and wound care facilities for early detection and management of diabetic foot complications.

Nutrition & Lifestyle Counselling

Dedicated counselling space providing structured meal planning, carb counting, and physical activity guidance — creating personalised, sustainable lifestyle strategies.

Comfortable Consultation Space

A calm, welcoming environment with private consultation bays designed to make your visit comfortable and stress-free — because managing diabetes starts with feeling at ease.
