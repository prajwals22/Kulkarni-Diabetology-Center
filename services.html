Comprehensive Diabetes Care

End-to-End Blood Sugar Management

Evidence-based, personalised management for every type of diabetes — from initial diagnosis through long-term glycaemic optimisation using CGM insights, newer medications, and individualised lifestyle plans.

📊
01
Advanced Treatment for Diabetes

Comprehensive Diabetes Assessment & Management

End-to-end management for all forms of diabetes using evidence-based protocols. We conduct a thorough metabolic evaluation — including HbA1c, fasting glucose, insulin levels, lipid profile, kidney function, and urine microalbumin — to paint a complete picture of your metabolic health. CGM insights, newer agent medications, and individualised lifestyle plans are integrated into a single personalised treatment roadmap designed specifically for you.

Best For:
All Forms of Diabetes Newly Diagnosed Annual Diabetes Review Poorly Controlled Diabetes Medication Optimisation
💊
02
Type 2 Diabetes

Type 2 Diabetes — Diagnosis, Optimisation & Prevention

Our Type 2 diabetes programme covers diagnosis, risk stratification, medication optimisation, weight management, and sustainable diet-activity planning with regular follow-up and complication screening built in. We use the latest oral and injectable agents — including SGLT-2 inhibitors and GLP-1 receptor agonists — to achieve tight glycaemic control while also protecting your heart and kidneys. Every plan is adapted to your lifestyle, comorbidities, and personal goals.

Best For:
Type 2 Diabetes Cardiovascular Risk Diabetic Kidney Disease Obesity with Diabetes Medication Review
👦
03
Type 1 Diabetes (Childhood-Onset)

Child & Family-Centred Type 1 Diabetes Care

Type 1 diabetes in children demands a holistic, family-centred approach. We provide insulin planning, education on carbohydrate counting, hypoglycaemia prevention, and school-readiness plans tailored to the child's age, activity level, and daily routine. Device support including CGM and insulin pump setup and training is offered, along with ongoing counselling for parents and caregivers to ensure confident day-to-day management at home and school.

Best For:
Type 1 Diabetes Children & Adolescents Insulin Pump Users Frequent Hypoglycaemia School-Age Management
🤰
04
Pregnancy & Diabetes

Gestational Diabetes — Trimester-Specific Care

Gestational diabetes demands the highest level of precision. We provide trimester-specific care in close collaboration with obstetricians, setting safe glucose targets, delivering structured dietary counselling, and ensuring consistent glucose monitoring throughout pregnancy. Following delivery, a postpartum reassessment is conducted to evaluate long-term risk and prevent future diabetes — protecting both mother and baby at every stage.

Best For:
Gestational Diabetes Pre-existing Diabetes in Pregnancy High-Risk Pregnancy Postpartum Diabetes Review
⚡
05
Prediabetes

Prediabetes — Early Intervention & Reversal

Prediabetes is a critical window of opportunity. Our early intervention programme focuses on weight control, structured dietary change, increased physical activity, sleep optimisation, and stress management — all proven to delay or prevent the onset of Type 2 diabetes. We track progress with periodic glucose and metabolic assessments, adjusting the programme as needed to keep your numbers moving in the right direction.

Best For:
Prediabetes Impaired Fasting Glucose Insulin Resistance Family History of Diabetes Weight Management
⚖️
06
Weight & Metabolic Health

Treatment for Obesity & Medical Weight Management

Excess weight is a complex metabolic disease, not simply a lifestyle issue. We provide a structured medical weight management programme tailored to your profile — combining nutrition planning, activity prescriptions, behaviour change support, and when appropriate, anti-obesity medications including GLP-1 agonists. Metabolic markers including blood sugar, blood pressure, lipids, and liver enzymes are monitored closely throughout your weight loss journey.

Best For:
Obesity with Diabetes Metabolic Syndrome Fatty Liver (NAFLD) Insulin Resistance Pre-Diabetes
Advanced Monitoring & Technology

Precision Technology for Better Control

State-of-the-art glucose monitoring, insulin delivery, and cardiovascular assessment tools — giving you and your doctor a complete, real-time picture of your metabolic health.

📡
01
Real-Time Glucose Technology

CGMS — Continuous Glucose Monitoring System

CGM has transformed diabetes management. A small sensor worn on the body measures glucose every few minutes, providing complete glucose trend data directly to your phone. We offer both short-term professional CGM (used in-clinic for diagnostic insights) and long-term personal CGM setups. Services include device selection, full initiation training, time-in-range optimisation, and regular data interpretation to make personalised adjustments to your meals, medications, and activity levels.

Best For:
Type 1 Diabetes Type 2 on Insulin Frequent Hypoglycaemia Poor HbA1c Control Pregnant Diabetics Insulin Pump Users
💉
02
Precision Insulin Delivery

Insulin Pump Therapy

Insulin pumps offer unparalleled flexibility and precision for patients requiring intensive insulin therapy. We provide a full pump service — from assessment for pump suitability and device selection, through initiation, initial settings optimisation, and comprehensive patient and family education. Ongoing support includes regular settings review, basal rate optimisation, and integration with CGM where appropriate to achieve a fully closed-loop or hybrid closed-loop diabetes management system.

Best For:
Type 1 Diabetes Brittle Diabetes Frequent Hypoglycaemia Gestational Diabetes Dose Optimisation
🫀
03
Cardiovascular Monitoring

ABPM — Ambulatory Blood Pressure Monitoring

A single clinic blood pressure reading can miss critical patterns. Ambulatory Blood Pressure Monitoring (ABPM) provides a full 24-hour BP profile, recorded automatically as you go about your normal daily activities and sleep. This allows us to diagnose masked hypertension, white-coat hypertension, and nocturnal dipping abnormalities with precision — refining your treatment, reducing cardiovascular risk, and protecting your kidneys from blood pressure-related damage.

Best For:
Hypertension with Diabetes White-Coat Hypertension Masked Hypertension Cardiovascular Risk Assessment Diabetic Nephropathy
🦋
04
Thyroid & Endocrine Care

Thyroid Disorder Management

The thyroid gland influences virtually every system in your body, and thyroid dysfunction is closely linked to metabolic and blood sugar disturbances. We provide comprehensive evaluation and management of all thyroid conditions through TSH, Free T3, Free T4, thyroid antibody testing, and thyroid ultrasound review. This includes management of hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, thyroid nodules, and pregnancy-related thyroid issues, with careful medication titration, symptom tracking, and structured long-term follow-up.

Best For:
Hypothyroidism Hyperthyroidism Hashimoto's Thyroiditis Thyroid Nodules Pregnancy Thyroid Issues Goitre
Complication Screening & Prevention

Protect Every Organ, Prevent Every Complication

Proactive screening, early intervention, and expert management of all diabetes-related complications — because prevention is always better than cure.

🦶
01
Diabetic Neuropathy

Diabetic Neuropathy Screening & Foot Care

Diabetic neuropathy is one of the most common and serious complications of long-standing diabetes. Our dedicated neuropathy screening programme includes foot risk assessment, vibration perception testing, monofilament sensory testing, and autonomic symptom review. Where neuropathy is identified, we provide targeted pain management strategies, foot-care education, and appropriate footwear advice to prevent ulcer formation. Early detection and intervention can prevent progression to limb-threatening complications.

Best For:
Peripheral Neuropathy Burning / Tingling Feet Foot Ulcer Prevention Annual Foot Screening Autonomic Neuropathy
🫘
02
Kidney Protection

Diabetic Nephropathy Screening & Management

Diabetic kidney disease is the leading cause of chronic kidney failure worldwide — yet it is largely preventable with early detection. We perform structured urine albumin checks, eGFR monitoring, and serum electrolyte testing at regular intervals to catch kidney involvement at the earliest possible stage. Our renal-protective treatment protocols — including ACE inhibitors, ARBs, and SGLT-2 agents as indicated — combined with blood pressure and glucose targets and renal-protective lifestyle guidance, are designed to slow or halt progression.

Best For:
Diabetic Nephropathy Microalbuminuria Chronic Kidney Disease Proteinuria Annual Kidney Screening
👁️
03
Eye & Heart Risk

Eye & Cardiovascular Risk Coordination

Diabetes is the most common cause of preventable blindness in working-age adults, and cardiovascular disease remains the leading cause of death in people with diabetes. We coordinate structured referral pathways for annual retinal examinations with our ophthalmology partners, alongside comprehensive lipid management, ECG and echocardiogram referrals when indicated, and vaccination guidance. Our aim is to protect your vision and your heart simultaneously through proactive, integrated specialist co-management.

Best For:
Diabetic Retinopathy Macular Oedema Cardiovascular Risk Dyslipidaemia Long-standing Diabetes
Nutrition, Lifestyle & Care Support

Treat the Root, Not Just the Number

Sustainable health transformation through structured nutrition therapy, lifestyle medicine, patient education, and coordinated specialist care — empowering you to take confident control of your diabetes.

01
Nutrition & Dietary Advice

Personalised Dietary Advice & Medical Nutrition Therapy

Food is the most powerful medicine in diabetes. We provide practical, culturally adaptable meal plans built around your preferences, food traditions, and daily routine. Our dietary counselling covers carbohydrate counting, the plate method, glycaemic index education, and strategies for festivals, restaurants, and travel. Individualized plans are crafted for vegetarians, working professionals, and athletes — so your nutrition plan fits your life, not the other way around.

Best For:
All Types of Diabetes Weight Management Dyslipidaemia Pre-Diabetes Reversal Gestational Diabetes

02
Activity & Habit Coaching

Exercise Prescription & Lifestyle Habit Coaching

Physical activity is a cornerstone of diabetes management — but the right prescription matters. We provide gradual, sustainable exercise plans that progress at a safe pace, incorporating strength training basics, daily movement goals, and aerobic activity targets appropriate for your fitness level and diabetes status. Alongside exercise, we address sleep hygiene and evidence-based stress reduction techniques that significantly influence blood sugar regulation and metabolic health.

Best For:
All Diabetes Types Sedentary Lifestyle Weight Management Stress-Related Hyperglycaemia Sleep Disorders

03
Patient Empowerment

Diabetes Self-Management Education & Counselling

Knowledge is the most powerful tool in diabetes. Our one-on-one education sessions cover everything you need to take confident control — insulin injection technique, hypoglycaemia prevention and treatment, sick-day rules, fasting and travel guidance, and how to interpret your own glucose readings. Families and caregivers are actively included. Educated patients consistently achieve dramatically better outcomes and experience fewer complications.

Best For:
Newly Diagnosed Insulin Starters CGM Users Families of Diabetics Poor Self-Management
