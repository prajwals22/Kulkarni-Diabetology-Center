Conditions We Treat

Comprehensive Metabolic Care
For Every Concern

Expert treatment for a wide range of diabetes, obesity, and metabolic conditions under one roof.

🩸

Type 1 Diabetes

Child and family-centred care with insulin planning, CGM, pump support, and hypoglycaemia prevention.

📊

Type 2 Diabetes

Diagnosis, medication optimisation, weight management, and sustainable diet-activity plans with regular follow-up.

⚡

Prediabetes

Early intervention with weight control, diet, activity, sleep, and stress management to delay or prevent diabetes.

🤰

Gestational Diabetes

Trimester-specific care in collaboration with obstetricians, with postpartum reassessment to reduce future risk.

🦋

Thyroid Disorders

Hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, thyroid nodules, and pregnancy-related thyroid issues — evaluation and management.

⚖️

Obesity & Metabolic Syndrome

Medical weight management combining nutrition, activity, behaviour change, and pharmacotherapy when indicated.

🦶

Diabetic Foot

Foot risk assessment, monofilament testing, pain management, and preventive foot-care education.

🫘

Diabetic Nephropathy

Urine albumin screening, eGFR monitoring, and renal-protective therapy to slow kidney disease progression.

👁️

Diabetic Retinopathy

Regular screening coordination and optimised glycaemic control to protect your vision from diabetic eye complications.

🫀

Diabetic Heart Risk

Lipid management, ABPM, and cardiometabolically protective therapy for integrated cardiovascular risk reduction.
